Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, technology, and digital devices in a safe, responsible, respectful, and ethical way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

An AUP is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use computers, the internet, and other technology, especially at a school or workplace.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is copying or using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work without giving them proper credit.

Plagiarism is taking someone else's work and pretending it is your own.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty means cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork or academic activities.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

AI-generated content rules explain how students can use AI tools responsibly, honestly, and appropriately for schoolwork.
